Aroom, my favorite Viet cafe in Seattle

August 17, 2026 · Aroom · Seattle Waterfront & Fremont

My favorite Viet cafe in Seattle.

This is from their Fremont location. Kevin ordered the banana coffee. He said the latte was super creamy, with a perfectly balanced banana flavor!

I ordered the banana matcha. I also love how creamy the matcha is. The foam on top is a little thicker than you'd usually expect, but it's so good mixed with the matcha. Not too sweet, and the matcha is not lost in any way. So yum.

I love their cups and the meaning behind their cafe. If you're in Seattle you should stop by!! I've stopped by multiple times, and one of these photos is from their waterfront location, right by the pier!

They've had some cool cafe DJ sets in the past. Good music and good drinks, what else could you ask for.

I love this cafe. I need to come back to try their food, maybe next summer.
