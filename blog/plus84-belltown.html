Plus84 in Belltown

July 13, 2026 · Plus84 · Belltown, Seattle

A Vietnamese minimalist cafe open till midnight.

Matcha with salted cream, 7.5/10. The matcha had a good earthy flavor and a natural sweetness. The salted cream on top wasn't sweet at all, which balanced it out okay.

Kettle corn coffee, 7/10. Lucia ordered this one. She said the coffee was strong (as expected with Vietnamese coffee) and not too sweet.

The place is a bit pricey, I paid $9.75 for a 12 oz matcha, but I would go back to try the other fun drinks they have, and being right by the pier is a plus.
