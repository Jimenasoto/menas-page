Le Grey in the International District

August 16, 2026 · Le Grey · Chinatown, Seattle

I loved the interior of this spot! Le Grey is a modern, minimalist bubble tea and specialty tea cafe in Seattle's Chinatown-International District.

Matcha cloudo, 8.5/10. This has been the best matcha I've had in Seattle. The salted cold foam paired perfectly with the rich matcha flavor throughout. Look at that color!

Mango kinu. @miilaasoto ordered this. So good, and in my opinion better than Hey Tea's coconut mango boom drink, a little bit sweeter. I think adding coconut would make it. Overall it's a perfect balance of sweetness and I loved the fresh mango pieces.

Kinda sad I tried this spot on my last day in Seattle. If I come back I'll definitely return.
